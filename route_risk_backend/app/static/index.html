Data sources & methods
  • Routing: OpenRouteService — driving-hgv profile (heavy goods vehicle, weight/height-restricted)
  • Road attributes: Digiroad WFS — speed limits, road width, lighting (Väylävirasto, CC BY 4.0)
  • Road weather: Digitraffic Weather — surface, friction, road/air temp, wind (Fintraffic, CC BY 4.0)
  • Winter maintenance: Digitraffic Maintenance Tracking — ploughing, salting, sanding, ice removal, last 24 h (Harja/Väylävirasto, CC BY 4.0)
  • Traffic incidents: Digitraffic Traffic Messages (Fintraffic, CC BY 4.0)
  • Darkness / twilight: Astral solar elevation model (Europe/Helsinki TZ)
  • Wildlife (moose) risk: LUKE seasonal collision statistics
  • HGV stopping distance: physics — d = (tr+tlag)v + v²/(2µg), tr=1.0 s, tlag=0.5 s air-brake; µ per surface from SWOV / Finnish road-friction studies
  • Map tiles: © OpenStreetMap contributors
